Key Clauses in a One-Way NDA Agreement Template While specific terms vary by document type, most unilateral non-disclosure agreements include these essential elements: * **Identification of Parties** — full legal names and addresses of all involved parties * **Effective Date** — when the agreement becomes enforceable * **Terms and Conditions** — the core obligations, rights, and responsibilities * **Duration and Termination** — how long the agreement lasts and how it can be ended * **Payment Terms** — amounts, due dates, late fees, and accepted payment methods * **Confidentiality** — protection of sensitive information (where applicable) * **Governing Law** — which state or jurisdiction's laws apply * **Signatures** — legally binding signatures from all parties ##
